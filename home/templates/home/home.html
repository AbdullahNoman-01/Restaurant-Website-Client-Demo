HERITAGE & ARTISANAL ETHOS

Our Story & Culinary Philosophy

♦

Founded in 1884 within a restored Grade II* listed Victorian banking edifice on King Street, The Chesterfield marries grand architectural heritage with daring contemporary British gastronomy.

Executive Chef Arthur Pendelton works hand-in-hand with heritage livestock breeders, small-boat Cornish fishermen, and Cheshire organic growers to celebrate the rich tapestry of the British seasons.
